Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what data Drill Helper (operated by Telera Engineering EOOD, "we", "us") collects and how it's used.

1. What we collect

  • Device identifier - a serial number generated on your device, used to track your credit balance. No account or personal registration is required.
  • Location data - the GPS coordinates or address you submit for analysis.
  • Photos - if you attach GPR device photos to an analysis, they are sent to our AI analysis provider for that specific request.
  • Purchase data - handled by Paddle.com Market Ltd, our payment provider and merchant of record. We receive confirmation of successful purchases and a device identifier; Paddle handles your payment details directly and we never see or store your card information.

2. How we use it

Location and photo data submitted for analysis is sent to our AI analysis provider to generate a result, and is not used for any other purpose. Your device identifier and credit balance are stored in our cloud database (Google Firebase) to keep your balance in sync if you reinstall the app. Past analyses (including any submitted photos and results) are stored on your device only, in local history, unless you choose to share or export a report yourself.

3. Third parties

We share data with the following processors, only as needed to provide the App:

  • Our AI analysis provider (processes location/device/photo data for each analysis request)
  • Paddle.com Market Ltd (payment processing, as merchant of record)
  • Google Firebase (cloud storage for credit balance sync)

4. Data retention

Your device's credit balance record is retained in our cloud database for as long as you use the App. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, or deletion of, the data associated with your device identifier by contacting us at the email below.
