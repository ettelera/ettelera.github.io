Pricing

Last updated: October 2026

Drill Helper uses a simple pay-as-you-go credit system. There is no subscription and no recurring charge. Every new device receives a free credit, so you can run a real analysis before buying anything.

Important notice. Drill Helper’s analyses are AI-generated, indicative estimates based on the location, publicly available geological information and, optionally, radar images you provide. They are not a guarantee that water will be found, nor of any depth, flow rate or water quality, and they do not replace the assessment of a licensed hydrogeologist or an on-site investigation. All drilling decisions and their costs are your responsibility.
