Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Credits purchased in Drill Helper are sold through Paddle.com Market Ltd, who acts as merchant of record for all purchases and handles billing on our behalf.

Requesting a refund

If you'd like to request a refund, contact Paddle directly, referencing your order confirmation email: paddle.net, or reach out to us at the email below and we'll help coordinate with Paddle.

Our policy

Drill Helper gives every new device a number of free trial credits before any purchase is required, so you can try a real analysis and evaluate the App before buying. Because of this, purchased credits are generally non-refundable once bought, with the following exceptions:

  • A duplicate or mistaken charge (for example, the same purchase processed twice).
  • A purchase affected by a proven technical fault on our side that prevented you from receiving or using the credits you paid for.
  • Where required by applicable consumer protection law in your country.

Note that as our merchant of record, Paddle reviews and decides all refund requests according to their own buyer policies, which may apply in addition to the above.

Separately: if an individual analysis attempt fails (for example, the AI service returns an error), no credit is deducted from your balance in the first place - a credit is only spent once a result is successfully delivered, so there is nothing to refund in that situation.
